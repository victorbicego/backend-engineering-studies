## Exercises
- [ ] Start a brag document today: write down every week what you delivered and its impact.
- [ ] Ask your manager: "What am I missing to reach senior or staff here?" Ask for concrete examples and review every quarter.
- [ ] Prepare 8 STAR stories: conflict, failure, leadership, hard decision, incident, mentoring, influence, delivery under pressure.
- [ ] Research salary bands for your target market on levels.fyi, Glassdoor and the Stack Overflow survey.
- [ ] Do at least 2 interviews a year at other companies to measure your market value.
